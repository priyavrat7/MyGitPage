My story

Research Software Developer with Full-Stack, Database, and DevOps Expertise

At MCIN, I develop and maintain LORIS, an open-source biomedical informatics platform for neurological research. I work across full-stack feature engineering, database administration, infrastructure configuration, release validation, and security-focused QA.

My review of role based access control and session handling in LORIS led to two security advisories that I reported and that are credited to me, now published and fixed in LORIS 27.0.4 and 28.0.2. I have 36 merged pull requests across LORIS and its CBIG project fork, covering imaging uploads, user accounts, candidate parameters, instrument forms, SQL project setup and security-fix backports, and I filed more than 20 defects during release QA.

I also contribute to open source networking and cloud tooling, with seven pull requests to Cisco pyATS and Genie (genielibs), the Meraki Dashboard API Python library, the Cisco AI Defense Python SDK and k8s-objectmatcher. Four are merged, including a pyasn1 pin update for known denial of service CVEs, and two more are approved and waiting on a final review.

My networking background comes from my graduate research at McGill, where I designed routing heuristics for collaborative UAV networks and compared AODV, DSDV and DSR in the NS2 simulator, and from teaching networking fundamentals (OSPF, BGP, VLANs, L2 and L3 switching, packet analysis in Wireshark). For packet capture, measurement and troubleshooting I use Wireshark, tshark, tcpdump, nmap, Scapy, iperf3, mtr, hping, netstat, ping and traceroute, and I design and test topologies in Cisco Packet Tracer.

Outside work, I built and maintain hospiaid.com, a live Laravel website and admin panel for a medical equipment and hospital setup company. Before MCIN, I worked at Ericsson on distributed ML training and edge device streaming, and as a freelance IoT engineer with Techamana.
